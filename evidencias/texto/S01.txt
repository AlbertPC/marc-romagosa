MYSTERY SHOPPING SERVICES MARKET SIZE AND FUTURE OUTLOOK

The global mystery shopping services market size was valued at USD 2.31 billion in 2025 and is projected to grow from USD 2.42 billion in 2026 to USD 3.61 billion by 2034, exhibiting a CAGR of 5.12% during the forecast period. North America dominated the mystery shopping services market with a market share of 43.89% in 2025.

Mystery shopping services are a method in which mystery shopping companies send individuals, known as secret shoppers or mystery shoppers, to visit sales locations, such as retail stores, hotels, and showrooms. These secret shoppers act as typical consumers, and they perform tasks, such as buying products or services, lodging complaints, and asking questions. In addition, this method helps companies check the overall quality of customer experience, offering businesses valuable insights and also helps in checking whether the employees are following the company’s procedure or not.

Prominent players operating in the mystery shopping services market include Netrika, BARE International, BestMark, IntelliShop, Signature Worldwide, and Sinclair Customer Metrics.

MYSTERY SHOPPING SERVICES MARKET TRENDS

Rise in Advancements at the Technological Level is the Major Trend Boosting Market Growth

With the rise in technology level, mystery shopping is witnessing a great transformation in evaluating and enhancing the customer journey. The increase in the adoption of technology, such as Augmented Reality (AR) platforms, integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI), mobile-enabled platforms, and video-based evaluations by mystery shopping service companies, has helped them collect more insights and understand customer requirements. Furthermore, artificial intelligence is considered to be the most significant trend in the mystery shopping industry. AI-powered algorithms study a large amount of data to recognize patterns and deliver unique insights. In addition, machine learning algorithms help in studying customer interactions, extracting their opinions and customer feedback, and pinpointing the areas of improvement. The growing adoption of video-based evaluations by mystery shoppers has helped them improve accountability and transparency as it serves as strong evidence for their insights. Thus, the surge in the adoption of advanced technologies by mystery shoppers will further fuel market growth.

North America witnessed mystery shopping services market growth from USD 1.02 billion in 2025 to USD 1.06 billion in 2026.

MARKET DYNAMICS
MARKET DRIVERS

Rise in Awareness Regarding Mystery Shopping Services Among Various Industries to Fuel Market Growth

The increase in awareness among various industries regarding the benefits of mystery shopping services, such as rising customer loyalty, improving customer service, recognizing strengths and areas for improvement, improving sales, and improving staff behavior, is driving the global mystery shopping services market growth. These services in a hotel, retail, restaurant, and automotive environment have increased significantly as they help in understanding the customer's expectations and also help in taking the necessary remedial actions. Furthermore, a rise in social media advertisements by mystery shopping service companies on digital platforms, such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn, has further increased the mystery shopper's awareness among various industries across the globe in a short period. Thus, the surge in awareness regarding mystery shopping services and increased social media advertisements by these companies globally drive the market growth.

MARKET RESTRAINTS

Presence of Insufficient Data to Restrain Market Growth

The presence of insufficient data and the risk of misinterpretation by mystery shoppers can lead to inaccurate information and data. The inaccurate data leads to reduced lead generation, which affects overall customer experience, declines revenue, and negatively affects the revenue forecast. Furthermore, the insufficient data makes it challenging to develop targeted marketing campaigns, customer retention, new product development, and social media advertisements. Thus, the lack of data and information from mystery shopping service providers results in restraining the growth of the industry.

MARKET OPPORTUNITIES

Sustainability and Ethical Shopping to Favor Market Expansion

Across the globe, consumers are becoming environmentally conscious and prefer brands with sustainable and ethical practices. Key companies, such as BARE International, BestMark, and Signature globally, are evolving to provide insights related to sustainable practices as a core part of consumer experience evaluation. This also includes measuring transparency in the supply chain, evaluating retailer's adherence to sustainability commitments, and also auditing their carbon footprints. Furthermore, the mystery shoppers also evaluate real-time blockchain data, which allows shoppers to trace the complete product lifecycle from production to the point of sale. Thus, increasing mystery shopping services that provide data related to sustainable products and ethical audits to industry players will further drive market growth.

MARKET CHALLENGES

High Service Costs to Impede Market Growth

The high costs associated with mystery shopping service companies are impacting the market growth. The service cost of mystery shopping service providers depends on various factors, such as the number of shoppers, the type of business, and the complexity of the task, which result in enhancing the service cost. Furthermore, continuous changes in consumer behavior, new technology developments, and shifting industry landscapes are factors that boost service costs and negatively impact industry growth.

IMPACT OF COVID-19

The COVID-19 pandemic negatively impacted the mystery shopping services industry. The complete closure of national and international boundaries, shutting down of all retail outlets, restaurants, and offices, implementation of lockdown measures across the countries, lack of availability of workforce, and restriction in the movement of goods and raw materials restricted the global market growth. Furthermore, the market began improving in 2021 as the government started uplifting the lockdown measures, which resulted in enhancing the production level and also in enhancing footfall in retail outlets.

BY TYPE

The In-Person Mystery Shopping Segment Dominated the Market Owing to Its Increase in Usage in Several Industries

Based on type, the market is segmented into video mystery shopping, in-person mystery shopping, telephone mystery shopping, online mystery shopping, and multiple touchpoint mystery shopping.

The in-person mystery shopping segment is expected to dominate the market share in 2026 by 33.47%. In-person mystery shopping is a practice in which mystery shoppers visit businesses as regular consumers to evaluate their consumer service and experience. Furthermore, in-person mystery shopping is used in various industries such as healthcare, hotel, automotive, retail, restaurant, banking, fitness, and petro-convenience.

The online mystery shopping segment is projected to rise with the highest CAGR of 6.16% during the forecast period. Advancements in technology have made online mystery shopping more important as it helps evaluate the aspects of the customer experience on mobile apps, websites, online platforms, and other websites.
