Acerca de las cookies
Las cookies son pequeños archivos de texto que las páginas web pueden utilizar para hacer más eficiente la experiencia del usuario.

La ley afirma que podemos almacenar cookies en su dispositivo si son estrictamente necesarias para el funcionamiento de esta página. Para todos los demás tipos de cookies necesitamos su permiso.

Esta página utiliza tipos diferentes de cookies. Algunas cookies son colocadas por servicios de terceros que aparecen en nuestras páginas.

En cualquier momento puede cambiar o retirar su consentimiento desde la Declaración de cookies en nuestro sitio web.

Obtenga más información sobre quiénes somos, cómo puede contactarnos y cómo procesamos los datos personales en nuestra Política de privacidad.

Al contactarnos respecto a su consentimiento, por favor, indique el ID y la fecha de su consentimiento.

General

¿Quiénes son tus compradores misteriosos?

Mystery shopping (also known as customer experience research) is a research method where customers from the target group visit a store or webshop and evaluate their experience. Mystery shoppers, if possible, make a purchase and fill out a detailed questionnaire after the visit, assessing the customer experience.

¿Cómo se seleccionan los compradores misteriosos?

Our mystery shoppers are real customers from the target group and service area, not professionals. We have a community of over 75,000 mystery shoppers, primarily in the Benelux, but active in more than 20 countries worldwide.   

They receive, if possible, shopping credit to spend directly in a store or webshop and fill out a detailed questionnaire after the visit. This way, they only sign up for brands they are interested in. Organizations receive authentic feedback from their target group about all aspects of the customer journey.   

¿Qué significa el precio por visita?

Esta es la tarifa que un cliente paga a Secret View.

¿Qué es el crédito para tiendas?

Para grandes organizaciones (más de 5 ubicaciones): Entre 27,50 € y 59,99 € por cliente misterioso (sin incluir el crédito de compra). La investigación es totalmente personalizada y el precio depende del número de ubicaciones, la frecuencia de las visitas, la complejidad del estudio y cualquier acuerdo adicional. Solicita una demostración o contáctanos para obtener un presupuesto a medida.  

Para pymes (de 1 a 5 ubicaciones): Entre 29,99 € y 49,99 € por cliente misterioso (sin incluir el crédito de compra). El precio varía según la extensión del cuestionario predefinido. No tienes compromiso de suscripción y solo pagas por cada tarea completada.   

¿Cómo se verifican las compras de los compradores misteriosos?

El crédito de la tienda siempre debe gastarse en su totalidad para que se devuelva como ingreso. Por eso pedimos a los compradores misteriosos que suban sus recibos al cuestionario. Esto te da una visión clara de sus compras.

¿Cómo garantizan la privacidad de mis empleados y clientes?

Shopping credit is the amount that mystery shoppers receive from organizations to spend directly in a store or webshop. This way, they only sign up for brands they are interested in and automatically belong to the target group.   

The shopping credit returns as revenue at the cash register, and mystery shoppers spend an average of 30% more than their credit. In some cases, we work with a financial compensation without purchase, for example, during advisory conversations or services.   

¿También puedo realizar una investigación sobre compras misteriosas en línea?

¡Absolutamente! Realizamos tareas en línea para varios clientes, incluidas las entregas de comestibles, comidas y bebidas alcohólicas. ¿Quiere obtener más información? ¡Reserva una demostración gratuita!

Compradores misteriosos

¿Cómo puedo obtener una tarea?

Crea una cuenta, inicia sesión en la plataforma y selecciona una tarea cerca de ti. Espera a que te confirmen que has sido seleccionado. Una vez aprobado, ¡lee detenidamente el resumen y completa la tarea en la fecha elegida! Además, hay tareas instantáneas que puedes realizar de forma inmediata. ¡Inicia sesión y comprueba si hay una disponible cerca de ti!

¿Cuándo me pagarán?

Depende de la asignación. Para la mayoría de las asignaciones de créditos para tiendas, se le paga por adelantado. Sin embargo, asegúrese de gastar el monto total.

¿Por qué tengo que verificar mi identidad?

Por varias razones. En primer lugar, debemos cumplir con leyes y reglamentos como el DAC7. Esto requiere que compartamos los detalles del pago con las autoridades fiscales, lo que significa que necesitamos verificar tu identidad. Esto no significa necesariamente que debas pagar impuestos por tus ganancias de Secret View. En la mayoría de los casos, tiene poco o ningún impacto en tu declaración de impuestos, pero depende de tu situación personal.

Do I need experience to get started?

No. You will always receive a briefing and a questionnaire, so you know exactly what to look for. If you can clearly describe situations and provide honest, constructive feedback, you’re already off to a great start.

How quickly do I need to complete the questionnaire?

Within 24 hours after your visit to the location. That way, everything is still fresh in your memory and we can share your feedback with the location as quickly as possible.

When do I get paid?

This varies per assignment:
‍Assignments with shopping credit: you usually receive the shopping credit in advance and spend the full amount during your visit.
‍Instant assignments: payment is made at the next payment moment, which is the first Monday after your assignment; it usually appears in your account within 1–2 days.
‍Assignments with a financial compensation: payment is made once your questionnaire has been approved; it usually appears in your account within 1–2 days.

How much do I earn per assignment?

This varies per assignment. In the platform, you always see the reward in advance. For example, shopping credit to purchase a product or service, or a financial compensation. That way, you know exactly what to expect before you apply.

Why do you do an ID check?

For several reasons:
- We must comply with laws and regulations (such as DAC7), which require identity verification. This does not automatically mean you have to pay tax; that depends on your personal situation.
- You receive a reward for your assignment, and sometimes we need to be able to reclaim shopping credit if an assignment is not (properly) completed.
- For compliance checks (alcohol/tobacco), we sometimes specifically look for 18- and 19-year-olds.

Dashboard

What do I see in the dashboard?

You get a clear overview of how your customer journeys and processes are performing. With just a few clicks, you can zoom in from a management-level view to specific locations and questions, all the way down to the individual questionnaire answers filled in by mystery shoppers or auditors.

How quickly do results appear in the dashboard?

In most cases, results are available in the dashboard within 24 hours after the visit. This helps you follow up fast, make adjustments, and coach teams in a targeted way.

How does segmentation work (and why is it so useful)?

With segmentations, you create your own “layers” in the data, for example by region, district managers, store types, or other custom segments. This makes it easy to see what’s going well, where teams need support, and where training will have the biggest impact.

What can I do with benchmarking?

Benchmarking lets you compare locations or segments by research round, customer journey, category, or question. It’s a quick way to spot top performers and improvement areas without endless searching.

What are rankings, and what do I use them for?

Rankings show the highest and lowest scores across different levels (for example locations, regions, or questions). You can click through to the underlying answers, which is great for targeted coaching and also fun for positive competition, like a store challenge.

Can I click through to qualitative feedback and examples?

Yes. You can drill down to the qualitative answers from mystery shoppers (or auditors). These real examples are useful for training, coaching, and evaluations.

Can I export results or connect to BI tools?

Yes. You can easily export results to Excel and PDF. For larger organisations, there’s an API to integrate with BI systems like Tableau, SAP, and Power BI.

Our community

Who is in your mystery shopping community?

Our community consists of over 65,000 real consumers. Not trained professionals, but people of all ages who go through your customer journey like real customers do.

In which countries is your community active?

The Secret View community is active in more than 20 countries, with a strong base in Europe. We match shoppers locally, so you get feedback that fits your region and target audience. Want to expand? We scale with you into other countries and build an approach together that delivers comparable, reliable insights everywhere.

How do you ensure the quality and reliability of the feedback?

Our mystery shoppers are 100% ID-verified, complete e-learnings, and build a reputation score. Every questionnaire also goes through AI-driven quality checks, so you can rely on consistent, actionable feedback.

How do you make sure mystery shoppers match our target audience?

We match per assignment based on target audience and region. Shoppers come from the area around the location, which makes the experience realistic and the feedback recognizable for your teams.

Why don’t you work with professional mystery shoppers?

Professional shoppers often visit locations all day and are more likely to stand out. That can create a biased view. With real consumers, you get feedback that aligns better with your target audience and customer journey.

How do you select the right mystery shopper for an assignment?

Mystery shoppers build a reputation score and profile as they complete assignments. We use these to select the best-fitting mystery shopper for each assignment, aligned with your target audience and location.

Why do you work with shop credit?

Where possible, we use shop credit instead of a financial payout. This encourages mystery shoppers to make a real purchase and choose assignments that genuinely fit them.

Pricing

What is the difference between Small Business and Enterprise?

Small Business is self-service: you can set up a research yourself within 15 minutes, with no subscription, and you can stop at any time. Enterprise is tailored: you get personal guidance, onboarding, support in 5 languages, and it’s suitable for high volumes (6 to 500+ locations).

What does “price per visit” mean?

A visit is one mystery shopping assignment.

With Small Business, you pay a fixed price per published assignment, based on the selected research frequency (weekly, biweekly, or monthly).

With Enterprise, we create a custom price after the demo, depending on factors like number of locations, frequency, and complexity.

Is there a subscription?

No. With Small Business, there is no subscription and you can cancel at any time. Enterprise is a tailored solution with agreements based on your situation.

Can I choose Enterprise (tailored) as a Small Business?

Yes. If you want a tailored questionnaire, setup, or guidance, you can request a free demo even as a smaller business to see what fits best.

How does payment work?

With Small Business, you pay per round via direct debit. For Enterprise, we agree on invoicing with you.

What is shop credit (and is it included in the price per visit)?

In many cases, we reward mystery shoppers with shop credit. Shop credit is not included in the price per visit and is charged on top of it. This shop credit is spent in your location(s) and therefore returns as revenue at the register.

The shop credit is based on the average receipt amount. In some situations, a financial reward without a purchase makes more sense, and that’s also possible.

How do you verify mystery shoppers’ spending?

Mystery shoppers upload the receipt in the questionnaire. This allows you to verify the purchase and see exactly what was bought.
